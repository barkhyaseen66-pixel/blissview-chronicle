# Blissview Chronicle

Build a professional, high-end, single-page book landing website for a novel titled "Lust and Love and the Difference Of" by Stephen Parks, published by Parker Publishers. I have attached the full book cover (back + spine + front). Use the FRONT cover as the hero image and match the website's visual identity to it.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Mood: dark, literary, vintage ink-sketch, emotional and cinematic. Think premium indie-publisher book launch page.
- Colors: near-black charcoal (#0e0e0e / #151515) as main background, warm parchment cream (#e9e1d0) for text and highlights, a muted aged-silver/beige (#b9ad94) for borders and secondary text. Optional single soft accent: faded antique gold (#b89b5e) for buttons only.
- Texture: subtle paper-grain / ink-scratch overlay on dark sections, and a torn-paper cream edge divider between sections (inspired by the cover's torn white shape).
- Typography: hand-lettered brush script for the main title and section headings (Google Font such as "Kaushan Script" or "Caveat Brush"), elegant serif for body text ("Cormorant Garamond" or "EB Garamond"), small-caps serif for labels and buttons.
- Decorative elements: the heart-with-cross symbol and the small ornamental flourish from the back cover as section dividers; thin ornate chain/vintage border frame around the blurb section.
- Fully responsive (mobile first), smooth scroll, subtle fade-in and parallax animations, no clutter.

SECTIONS
1. Sticky navbar: small heart-symbol logo + title, links: About, Characters, Themes, Author, Get the Book. Transparent on top, dark blur on scroll.
2. Hero: large front cover image with soft glow and slight 3D tilt on hover; title in brush script; tagline "Some places become memories. Blissview University becomes home."; buttons "Buy the Book" and "Read the Story". Show "By Stephen Parks".
3. About the Book (inside a vintage bordered frame), using this text:
   "For Sophia, being at Blissview is more than a college education. Surrounded by loyal friends, inspiring mentors, and a campus unlike any other, she discovers that family isn't always something you're born into. Sometimes it's something you find.
   But as Sophia's bond with Jonathan Thomas grows into something neither of them can ignore, they find themselves walking a path where love and duty collide. Every choice carries consequences, and the closer they become, the greater the risk of losing everything they've worked to build.
   Filled with unforgettable friendships, humor, heartbreak, and hope, Lust and Love, and the Difference Of is a sweeping novel about found family, impossible choices, and discovering that the greatest moments in life are often built one act of kindness at a time."
4. Meet the Characters: two elegant cards for Sophia and Jonathan Thomas (short placeholder descriptions I can edit later), styled like ink-sketch portraits with cream borders.
5. Themes: four icon cards — Found Family, Love vs Duty, Friendship & Humor, Heartbreak & Hope.
6. Book Details: format, publisher (Parker Publishers), ISBN 978-1-963452-08-7, genre (Contemporary Romance / Campus Fiction), with the barcode style shown subtly.
7. Reader Reviews: carousel with clearly marked placeholder testimonials that I will replace.
8. About the Author: Stephen Parks, with image placeholder and short editable bio.
9. Get the Book: buy buttons (Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Publisher site) with placeholder links, plus a newsletter signup form ("Join the Blissview community").
10. Footer: Parker Publishers logo area, social icons, copyright, ISBN.

REQUIREMENTS
- Clean, well-organized React + Tailwind code, reusable components, good accessibility (alt texts, contrast, focus states), fast loading, SEO meta tags and Open Graph tags using the book title and tagline.
- Do not invent fake awards or reviews; keep all placeholders clearly editable.
- Keep the overall look consistent: dark, cream, vintage ink.
